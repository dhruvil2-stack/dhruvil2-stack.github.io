Bugs I’ve chased down

Short write-ups on things that broke and how I worked them out.

  • Oct 2026Alternating tone pairs so echoes miss the receiverThe modem now hops between 54/57 kHz and 55/61 kHz on every other symbol. An echo from one symbol lands on the pair we aren’t listening to.
  • Oct 2026Two resonances, each hiding the otherOur transducer had a weak second mode buried under the main one. Peeling them apart in MATLAB took the model from 18% error to 3%.
  • Oct 2026Why my MP3 player kept resettingThe supply dipped every time I skipped a song. A bigger capacitor fixed most of it.
  • Oct 2026Finding the usable band: about 45–60 kHzWe tested carriers in the pool instead of trusting the 65 kHz on the spec sheet.
  • Sep 2026Our 65 kHz transducers were ringing at 149 kHzA hand calculation matched it to the length mode of the tube.
